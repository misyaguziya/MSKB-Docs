**MSKB v1.0.2** is out. When you type fast, pressing the gap between two keys no longer types nothing. The symbol page's "Recent" tab is also kept after MSKB restarts.

<!-- truncate -->

## Improvements

### The gaps between keys can be pressed

There is a gap between keys, and pressing it typed nothing. When typing fast, a key you thought you had pressed sometimes did not go in.

From v1.0.2, pressing a gap presses the nearer key. The keys look the same as before.

### "Recent" on the symbol page is kept

The symbol page's "Recent" tab (the symbols and emoji you entered last) was cleared when MSKB restarted. From v1.0.2 it is kept after a restart: up to 22, newest first.

See [Symbols and emoji](/input/symbols) for details.

### "Updates" has its own section in the settings

The settings window's "General" page now has an "Updates" section at the bottom. "Check for updates at startup" is switched on and off there. Its description says what is checked, and that MSKB only tells you and never downloads.

See [New version notice](/settings/pc#update-notice) for details.
