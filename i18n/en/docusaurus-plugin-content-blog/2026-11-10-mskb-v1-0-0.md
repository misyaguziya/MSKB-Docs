**MSKB v1.0.0**, a multilingual VR keyboard for VRChat, is out. Type without taking off your headset, and send to the chatbox or into VRChat's text fields.

<!-- truncate -->

## What it does

- **Typing**: Japanese (romaji and flick), English, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Pinyin, Zhuyin, Cangjie and Quick), and voice input
- **Symbols**: symbols, brackets, shapes, arrows and numbers, emoji, kaomoji, and saved phrases
- **Editing**: moving the cursor, selecting, copy and paste, undo, and recalling what you sent
- **Sending**: to VRChat's chatbox, or into VRChat's text fields
- **Look and sound**: colors, opacity, an image behind the keys, a sound for each key, and vibration

The settings screen and the keyboard can be shown in Japanese, English, Korean, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese.
